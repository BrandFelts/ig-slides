## Confidence framework topics
From Brand's guide "The Brand Felts Approach to Confidence". Each subsection below is one carousel. Use his wording. Where it fits, the hook or caption can mention "The Brand Felts Approach to Confidence". Some lines overlap with "Embarrassment and fear aren't real" above, so don't post both in the same week.

### Confidence through ability
- The "just be confident, bro" mentality is nothing more than a superficial, surface-level fix
- Anyone can fake confidence outwardly for a while
- Unearned confidence comes with fragility and instability. It can be shattered at any moment
- True confidence doesn't come from deciding one day that you'll be confident. It comes from proof and ability
- Be "confidence worthy"

### Proof x ability
- Proof is evidence that you are who you say you are, and who you want to be
- Proof builds trust, not only with others but with yourself, which is more important
- When you trust yourself, other people can't take your confidence from you
- If you're not where you want to be in your fitness, spiritual, financial or other goals, any confidence you have is unearned
- Your ability needs to match your level of confidence
- Proof x ability ÷ embarrassment = confidence. If proof or ability is zero, confidence is zero

### Embarrassment is an optional emotion
- Embarrassment is your mind playing tricks on you. It's not real
- Any time you were embarrassed, you chose to be, and that's your fault
- Embarrassment is just the discomfort you feel based on how you believe others perceive you
- Detach yourself from other people's opinions of you
- Most people are subconsciously living to please others and have no idea
- When you start caring more about the trajectory of your life than about what people may think of you, your new life starts

### Jeremy from accounting
- The opinions that keep you from living the life you want probably come from people you don't care much about
- Who cares what Jeremy from accounting has to say about the new social media account you made, or the small business you tried to start?
- You won't be 80 years old wishing you had listened to him more
- Even family and friends won't pay your bills for you. They can't experience the world for you
- The most important opinion anybody has of you is the one you have of yourself

### Where do you stand? (4 types)
- Fake confident: loud, but nothing to back it up. The "just be confident, bro" guy
- Insecure: nothing to stand on, and afraid of what people think
- People pleaser: has the skills, but still lives for other people's approval
- Confidence worthy: has earned it, and doesn't need anyone's approval
- (Hook idea: "There are 4 types of people when it comes to confidence. Which one are you?")

### R.E.A.L (the 4 steps)
- My process to real confidence is R.E.A.L
- R: Realization. Own where you are, and that it's on you
- E: Embarrassment. Face it until it loses all its power
- A: Ability. Earn your confidence with real results
- L: Livin'. Live on your terms, free from others

### Realization
- You aren't who you want to be, and it's your fault
- You've let other people hijack your life to fit what they want from you
- You can't have real confidence because you suck
- Go on a long, hard self-improvement journey and become worthy of being confident
- You have the ability to do all of this, and you have the whole time

### Ability
- Become confidence worthy
- If you're out of shape, broke, lazy and uninteresting, you have nothing to be confident about
- If you're a powerhouse firing on all cylinders, you build confidence solid enough to stand in front of a train and cut it in half
- You can never have truly unwavering confidence if you suck

### True free will
- When you eliminate embarrassment and become a high achiever, you gain pure purpose
- When you carry your own destiny instead of other people's expectations, you unlock true free will
- People say they have free will when they go to a new coffee shop. I don't participate in quirky, half-assed stuff like that
- Free will is when you do what you want to do because you wanted to do it

### The 60-day Free Will Challenge
- Every day for 60 days:
- 1. One social interaction with a stranger
- 2. One risk that puts you in position to potentially be embarrassed or succeed
- Get embarrassed enough and it loses all its power

### Confidence tools
- Have one physical, one mental, one creative and one spiritual hobby
- The one-second decision: recognize the moment you actually decide, and choose what you know you should do, not the safer play
- Make a fool of yourself. Ask stupid questions, post videos for people to criticize, make small talk with strangers
- Be true to your word. Do what you say you will, and don't say you can do things you can't

### Hesitancy kills
- You'll have to get a little selfish, step on people's toes and put yourself first
- If you ever hesitate to do something solely out of fear of embarrassment, then you must do it
- Unlearn the habit of watering down your decisions to avoid being embarrassed
- Don't listen to the opinions of people who don't matter much to you
- Hesitancy kills
- If you really want this, you have to go all in
